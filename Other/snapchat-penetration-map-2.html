Figures are estimated penetration rates (% of population using Snapchat monthly), compiled from Data Reportal, EMARKETER, and third-party trackers — not official Snap Inc. breakdowns. Penetration tells a very different story than raw user counts: small, wealthy, mobile-first markets like Saudi Arabia, Norway, and the UAE lead by share of population, even though India and the U.S. have far more total users. Russia and China are marked as blocked rather than no-data — both governments restrict or ban Snapchat outright (Russia's block took effect in late 2025). Countries with a dashed yellow outline, including Estonia, Bahrain, and most of Sub-Saharan Africa and Central Asia, have no published country-level figure; trackers typically fold them into regional buckets ("Other Central & Eastern Europe," etc.) rather than reporting them individually, so no real number exists to show rather than an invented one.
