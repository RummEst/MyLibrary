— A different receptor entirely

Meanwhile: GABA-B

Everything above is GABA-A — a fast, ionotropic chloride channel. GABA also activates a completely unrelated receptor, GABA-B, which is metabotropic: no pore at all, just a slow chemical signal.

How it's built

GABA-B is a G-protein-coupled receptor that only works as a pair — an obligate heterodimer of GABA-B1 (which binds GABA) and GABA-B2 (which activates the G-protein). Neither subunit works alone.

What it does

Presynaptically, it closes voltage-gated calcium channels to throttle back neurotransmitter release. Postsynaptically, it opens GIRK potassium channels, producing a slow inhibitory current that lasts far longer than a GABA-A IPSP.

Pharmacology

Baclofen is a selective GABA-B agonist used clinically as a muscle relaxant for spasticity (e.g., in multiple sclerosis or spinal cord injury). Selective antagonists like CGP55845 exist only as research tools.
